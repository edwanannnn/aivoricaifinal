## Design Philosophy

Architecture is about space, light, and form. We translated these principles into digital:

- **Grid System:** Rigid yet flexible, echoing architectural blueprints
- **Typography:** Bold geometric sans-serifs paired with refined serifs
- **Motion:** Deliberate, architectural transitions between sections
- **Photography:** Directed specifically for web, emphasizing scale and detail

## Technical Approach

Custom-built with Astro and GSAP for maximum performance and animation fidelity. Lighthouse score: 98/100.
