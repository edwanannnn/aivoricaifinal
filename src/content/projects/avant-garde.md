## A Living Identity

The Avant Garde brand doesn't stand still—it evolves. We designed a flexible system that:

- **Adapts:** Logo variations for different contexts and exhibitions
- **Responds:** Color palette shifts with each season's programming
- **Engages:** Interactive digital touchpoints throughout the gallery
- **Connects:** Seamless integration with artist collaborations

## Digital Experience

The website serves as a gateway to the gallery, featuring virtual tours, exhibition previews, and an integrated ticketing system.
