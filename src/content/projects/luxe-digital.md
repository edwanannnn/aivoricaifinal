## The Challenge

Selling high-end furniture online presents unique challenges. Customers need to visualize pieces in their spaces, understand materials, and feel confident in significant purchases—all without physical interaction.

## Our Approach

We developed a digital experience that bridges the gap between online shopping and in-store luxury:

- **3D Visualization:** Full product rotation and zoom capabilities
- **AR Integration:** "View in Your Space" functionality for mobile devices
- **Material Library:** Detailed texture and fabric exploration
- **White Glove Service:** Seamless scheduling for delivery and installation

## Technical Excellence

Built on Shopify Plus with custom Hydrogen frontend, the platform handles high traffic while maintaining sub-second load times.
