The web design landscape is evolving rapidly. Here's what we're seeing in 2024.

## Key Trends

### AI-Powered Design
AI tools are augmenting (not replacing) designers, accelerating ideation and production.

### Micro-Interactions
Subtle animations that provide feedback and delight are becoming standard.

### Brutalist Revival
Raw, unpolished aesthetics are making a comeback among experimental brands.

### Horizontal Scrolling
When done right, horizontal scroll creates memorable, unique experiences.

### 3D and Immersive Web
WebGL and Three.js are bringing rich 3D experiences to browsers.

## What We're Watching

The intersection of AI and creativity is the most exciting space right now. We're carefully integrating AI tools into our workflow while keeping human creativity at the center.
