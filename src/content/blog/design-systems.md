A design system is more than a style guide—it's the foundation for scalable, consistent brand experiences.

## What is a Design System?

A design system is a collection of reusable components, guided by clear standards, that can be assembled to build any number of applications.

## The Benefits

### 1. Consistency
Every touchpoint feels cohesive, from your website to your app to your emails.

### 2. Efficiency
Stop reinventing the wheel. Components are built once and reused everywhere.

### 3. Scalability
New products and features launch faster when the foundation is solid.

## Key Components

- **Design tokens**: Colors, typography, spacing
- **Component library**: Buttons, forms, cards
- **Documentation**: How and when to use each element
- **Governance**: Who maintains and updates the system

Building a design system is an investment that pays dividends for years.
